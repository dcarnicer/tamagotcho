## What's new

- **Fix: a deck unreadable after the second download.** After two deck
  downloads in one session (Get decks, or the portal page, which also reads
  the catalog), the loaded deck could lose its name and its cards ("All caught
  up" on a deck never studied, kanji not drawn) until the device restarted.
  The firmware's TLS client closed descriptor 0 at the end of every HTTPS
  request, and after a download that was the deck's file. Every board was
  affected; it showed first on the Cardputer ADV.

**Updating from v0.4.0:** the app-only image keeps settings, decks and
progress (`write_flash 0x10000 tamagotcho-<board>.bin`).

**Checked on hardware** (2026-10-21): two and three downloads in one session
on a StickS3 (the `m5stack` image and a streamed-mode debug build), the deck
readable throughout.
