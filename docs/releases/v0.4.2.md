## What's new

- **Deck update checks.** With a home Wi-Fi saved, the device now reads the
  release catalog at boot and tells you when an installed deck has a newer
  build: once a day on USB power, or once a week on battery from 50 %. Decks
  shows "N updates" and a new **Updates** row lists only the decks with a new
  build; a hold downloads and replaces one in place, keeping your progress.
  The check is a short screen you can skip, and it never downloads by itself.
  It compares sizes in this version: a rebuild with the exact same byte count
  is not detected (hashing the installed file is the follow-up).
- **Weakest kanji in the portal.** The page's Statistics section now lists the
  ten kanji you get wrong most often (at least 3 reviews and one miss), with
  their right/review counts; `/api/stats` carries the same `trouble` array.
- **The published decks were rebuilt** (`decks-v2`, 2026-10-22) with common
  readings the Jōyō table leaves out (等 など, 経 た.つ, 被 かぶ.る). Get decks
  and the portal download them; a deck already installed shows the new build
  in **Decks > Updates**.

**Updating from v0.4.1:** the app-only image keeps settings, decks and
progress (`write_flash 0x10000 tamagotcho-<board>.bin`). The `m5stack` image on
M5Burner is one entry for the StickS3 and the Cardputer ADV. The new update
keys (`updday`, `updcount`, `updok`) start empty, so the first boot after the
update can check the catalog once.

**Checked on hardware** (2026-10-22):

- StickS3: the boot check (all decks up to date), the Updates row, and a
  deliberately replaced N4 (built without stroke order) detected, downloaded
  and replaced; the loaded N5 kept its 5 due / 67 new.
- Cardputer ADV: the keyboard drove the same flow, Get decks downloaded N4,
  the automatic boot check counted the replaced N4 (badge 1), and Enter
  updated it from the catalog. Its original M5Launcher flash was backed up and
  restored afterwards.
