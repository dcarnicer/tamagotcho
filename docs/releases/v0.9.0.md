## What's new

- **The Cardputer ADV draws in landscape again.** v0.8.0 started it at
  135×240 (portrait) with the old display stack; v0.9.0 uses the 240×135
  landscape layout with the keyboard below the picture, as intended. Checked
  on a real Cardputer ADV.
- **The StickS3 backlight is actually 25 kHz now.** The old core addressed
  the LEDC channel by number, so the frequency change silently missed the
  backlight; Arduino 3 addresses it by pin. The charger's inductor buzz on
  USB should be gone.
- **Arduino 3 under the hood.** The firmware moves to Arduino-ESP32 3.3.12
  (IDF 5.5.5) with M5Unified/M5GFX 0.3.0 and LovyanGFX 1.3.0. This is a
  maintenance release: **no new decks, no pack format change, no settings
  migration**. The flash layout is unchanged.
- **Updates from v0.8.0 are tested.** The old Arduino 2 bootloader was left
  in place and updated by OTA on a StickS3, a Cardputer ADV and a
  T-Display-S3; each booted v0.9.0 and kept settings, decks and progress.

**Checked on hardware** (2026-11-19): StickS3 and Cardputer ADV from v0.8.0,
and a T-Display-S3 from its previous build, all updated over the air with the
old bootloader. Wi-Fi, NTP, the TLS catalog, a deck download, the quiz,
rotation and the Cardputer keyboard/microSD were exercised; the full flash
was restored from a verified backup after each test. Native tests pass (304
cases, 1 skipped) and the pack builder passes 259 tests.

**Tooling**: the pack builder moves to Pillow 13 and pypdf 7.1 (the UI font
and the Jōyō table parse are unchanged), the embedded root CAs are refreshed
from certifi 2026.10.23 and JmdictFurigana is pinned to its 2026-10-25
release. These affect future deck builds only.

**Decks**: [`decks-v3`](https://github.com/dcarnicer/tamagotcho/releases/tag/decks-v3)
still holds the 24 packs and the catalog the device reads; rebuilding them is
not needed for this release.
