## What's new

- **One image for the StickS3 and the Cardputer ADV**:
  `tamagotcho-m5stack-merged.bin` (and the app-only `tamagotcho-m5stack.bin`)
  finds the board at boot. It is the image on M5Burner, where Tamagotcho has
  one entry for both boards. The single-board images stay.
- **On M5Burner**: search for Tamagotcho, connect a StickS3 or a Cardputer
  ADV and press Burn.
- **Network time tries twice**: when NTP does not answer within 3 s right
  after joining the home Wi-Fi, it asks once more.
- **Nothing cut short in portrait**:
  - Settings › Device › Date shows the date and the zone under their labels.
  - The Wi-Fi menu says "Forget Wi-Fi" (was "Forget network") and, in
    Spanish, "Configurar" (was "Modo configuración").
- **A new README** with real screens, and a user guide (`docs/user-guide.md`)
  for the long manual.

**Updating from v0.3.0:** the app-only image keeps settings, decks and
progress; a StickS3 or a Cardputer ADV can also move to the `m5stack` image
the same way (`write_flash 0x10000 tamagotcho-m5stack.bin`).

**Checked on hardware** (2026-10-21):
- The `m5stack` image on a StickS3 and on a Cardputer ADV (a deck download
  on the Cardputer), and its merged image on a blank Cardputer ADV.
- The fixes above on a StickS3 (the Date page and the Spanish Wi-Fi menu in
  portrait, NTP's second try on a Cardputer ADV).
