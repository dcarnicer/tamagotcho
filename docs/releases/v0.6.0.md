## What's new

- **Learning mode: new kanji are introduced before they are asked.** A kanji
  you have never seen gets an intro card first: its stroke order animating,
  its meanings (the one the quiz asks first, the others dimmed), its readings
  (the asked one of each kind first) and, with the new decks, two example
  words. Tap the front button to go on: the kanji is asked a few cards later,
  first its meaning, then a reading, and comes back the next day. New kanji
  arrive in batches of four (four intros, their meanings, their readings). If
  you already know it, tap the side button instead (**known**): it is asked at
  once as an ordinary first question. The intro is not an answer and does not
  count against your accuracy. On by default; **Settings › Study ›
  Introduce** turns it off. Kanji with progress from earlier versions are not
  introduced again.
- **Example words.** The decks now carry up to two common words per kanji
  that use its readings (水: 水曜日 すいようび "Wednesday", 水 みず "water"),
  with the part the kanji reads highlighted and a short meaning. English
  glosses come from JMdict; Spanish ones from a table reviewed by hand, N5 so
  far (the other Spanish decks show their words with the reading alone until
  their level is reviewed). The words are on a second page of the intro (the
  first tap turns to it), or under the readings when they fit (T-Display-S3,
  portrait).
- **New decks release, `decks-v3`.** The decks with words are pack format v3,
  in their own release; the default catalog now points at it. Decks already on
  the device keep working (without words), and **Decks › Updates** offers the
  new ones. A catalog URL you saved on the portal page is kept. `decks-v2`
  stays as it is for v0.5.1 and older.
- **The portal can replace and delete the loaded deck.** Uploading from the
  portal page a deck with the same file name as the loaded deck failed
  ("cannot store the deck"), and so did a URL download under that name: the
  device keeps the loaded deck's file open, and the filesystem refuses to
  replace an open file. Deleting the loaded deck from the page had the worse
  form of the same bug: it forgot the deck's progress and then kept the deck.
  The deck is now closed for the swap or the delete and reopened (or the next
  one loaded) afterwards; a failed swap reopens the old deck.
- **Under the hood:** `main.cpp` is split into one module per screen or area
  (`firmware/src/app/`), with no change in behaviour.

**Updating from v0.5.1:** the app-only image keeps settings, decks and progress
(`write_flash 0x10000 tamagotcho-<board>.bin`). The `m5stack` image on M5Burner
is one entry for the StickS3 and the Cardputer ADV. Progress belongs to each
kanji, so updating a deck to its v3 build keeps it.

**Checked on hardware** (2026-10-24, with the serial remote):

- **StickS3** (the intro card's branch): intros in Spanish and English, the
  words' page with the reading's span highlighted, batches of four, the
  meaning and reading steps, relearns after a miss, a counter that only goes
  down, the done screen's "+10 new", the settings and back, "known" from the
  words' page, **Introduce** Off and On, landscape, and a v2 deck (no words'
  page).
- **T-Display-S3**: the words under the readings in portrait (外国人 がいこくじん
  wraps its reading, nothing is lost), page 2 in landscape, a double tap that
  does not skip the next intro, "known", the orientation changed with an intro
  open. Through the portal, with the deck loaded: an upload under its name, a
  URL download (good and 404) and its delete, which loaded the next deck and
  kept the progress its kanji share with another deck.
- **Cardputer ADV**: a first boot, an upload, the intro's keys (Enter and →
  go on, ← is "known" on the words' page, Esc opens the settings and a second
  Esc returns to the same page),
  **Get decks** from `decks-v3` (12 decks; the loaded deck updated in place)
  and N4 in Spanish, whose words show their reading alone.

All four release environments build (`sticks3`, `tdisplay-s3`,
`cardputer-adv`, `m5stack`); native tests: 277 passed, 1 skipped.
