## What's new

- **A new name: たまごっちょ (Tamagotcho)**, a 電子単語帳 (denshi tangochō),
  an electronic flash-card book.
  - The header shows たまごっちょ.
  - The access point is `Tamagotcho-XXXX` and the portal answers at
    `http://tamagotcho.local`.
  - The release files are named `tamagotcho-*`.
  - Settings, decks and progress carry over from v0.2.1.
- **A third board: the M5Stack Cardputer ADV**, in landscape over its keyboard.
  - `;` `.` `,` `/` are the arrows. On the 2×2 answers they move by row and
    column.
  - Enter answers or chooses at once. Esc (`` ` ``) or del goes back. Tab
    opens the settings.
  - G0 is the front button, and any key wakes it from sleep.
  - It has no PSRAM, so a deck's tables are read from flash as they are
    needed, the whole Jōyō deck included.
- **On'yomi answers in hiragana**, for readers more used to it: Settings ›
  Study › On'yomi, Katakana or Hiragana.
  - Only the answer options change; the detail card after a wrong answer keeps
    katakana.
  - It needs the decks published on 2026-10-21: update yours in Get decks. In
    an older copy, some hiragana show as boxes.
- **Better reading questions.**
  - The decks put each kanji's common readings first, and a question asks the
    first one.
  - A kanji with no reading of the kind it was asked for gets the other kind.
  - A wrong option is never another reading of the kanji, of either kind or
    script.
- **Spanish meanings** follow the English order, so the first meaning is the
  one a learner expects, with the maintainer's corrections.
- **Delete progress**: Settings › Study › Delete progress, for this deck or for
  everything. Deleting a deck from the device deletes its progress too.
- **Date screen**:
  - A side tap goes back a day and a side hold cancels.
  - A day before today can be set.
- **Done screen**: no "+10 new" once every card of the deck has been seen.
- **Hint bar**: a second row when a screen's actions do not fit in one.
- **T-Display-S3**, checked on a real board:
  - It stays awake on a computer's USB; on a plain charger it sleeps as on
    battery.
  - Without an accelerometer, Orientation no longer reads Auto.

**Updating from v0.2.1:** the app-only image keeps settings, decks and
progress. Then open Get decks and update every deck: `decks-v2` was rebuilt on
2026-10-21 with the reading order, the meaning order and the hiragana glyphs.

**Checked on hardware** (flashed with PlatformIO):
- This firmware on a StickS3.
- The same day's builds, before the hiragana option, on a T-Display-S3 and a
  Cardputer ADV.
