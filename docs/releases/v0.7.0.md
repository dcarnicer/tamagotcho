## What's new

- **Firmware updates over Wi-Fi.** **Settings › Device › Firmware** shows the
  running version and, when a newer release is out, installs it with a hold:
  the device downloads its own build's image into its second app slot, checks
  the size and SHA-256 against the release's `tamagotcho-firmware.txt`, and
  restarts into it. Settings, decks and progress stay. A new version that
  does not start goes back to the previous one by itself, and that version is
  not offered again. The device looks for updates in the same Wi-Fi sessions
  as the deck updates (an amber gear on the **Device** row in portrait, an
  arrow in landscape) and on **Check now**; nothing installs without a hold.
  v0.7.0 is the first version that can update itself, so v0.7.1 will be the
  first to arrive over the air.
- **Decks on the Cardputer ADV's microSD card.** With a FAT32 card in, decks
  live in `/tamagotcho/decks/` on the card: Get decks and the portal write
  there, decks in the device's memory move to the card by themselves (each
  copy checked: size, SHA-256), and an **SD** pill marks them. The progress
  stays in the device's memory, with a copy in `/tamagotcho/progress.bin` at
  every sleep and restore. Pulling the card shows "Card removed", and the
  deck opens again when the card is back. exFAT cards (most over 32 GB) must
  be reformatted as FAT32. It works with the Cap LoRa-1262 attached, which
  shares the card's lines.
- **Less room for decks in the device's memory** on the 8 MB boards (StickS3,
  Cardputer ADV): the second app slot takes 1.5 MiB, leaving 4.44 MiB. N5 to
  N1 in one language fit; the Jōyō deck and N1 no longer fit together. The
  T-Display-S3 keeps 11.94 MiB.

**Moving to v0.7.0 (once, over USB).** The flash layout changes, so v0.7.0
cannot arrive over the air, and **the decks and progress in the device's
memory are not kept** ([user guide](https://github.com/dcarnicer/tamagotcho/blob/main/docs/user-guide.md#moving-to-v070-once)):

1. Before: portal page › **Advanced › Progress backup**, download the file.
2. Flash v0.7.0 with its partition table: the merged image
   (`write_flash 0x0 tamagotcho-<board>-merged.bin`, the install page or
   M5Burner), which also resets the settings and the home Wi-Fi; or, from a
   build, `pio run -e <board> -t upload`, which keeps them. An app-only update
   over v0.6.x keeps the old layout: the device works but cannot update itself.
3. After: **Get decks** again (on the Cardputer ADV, put a FAT32 card in
   first), then restore the progress file on the portal page.

**Checked on hardware** (2026-10-24, with the serial remote):

- **StickS3**: the move from v0.6.0 (progress backup, USB flash, settings and
  home Wi-Fi kept, progress restored and 8 decks uploaded into the 4.4 MiB).
  Updates from a manifest served on a computer: an image built to crash at
  boot rolled back to the previous version and was not offered again; three
  good images alternated between the two slots and were marked valid after
  10 s, with decks and progress intact; cancelling with the side and power
  buttons; a USB upload after an update booted the new image. The `m5stack`
  image (no card slot) listed and opened its decks as before.
- **T-Display-S3**: the move, an update into its 2 MiB slot, the Firmware
  screen in both orientations.
- **Cardputer ADV** (with the Cap LoRa-1262 on): a 32 GB FAT32 card (mounted
  in 8 ms, 15 KB of RAM); three decks moved to the card with the loaded one
  reopened from it; Get decks to the card, including an update of the loaded
  deck; the progress copy; pulling the card while studying and putting it
  back.
- Not tried yet: an update over the air on the Cardputer ADV (no PSRAM; the
  download uses the same TLS path as Get decks, which works there), an exFAT
  card's message, a card filling up during the move.

All four release environments build and fit their app slots (the largest,
`m5stack`, uses 86 % of its 1.75 MiB); native tests: 302 passed, 1 skipped.
