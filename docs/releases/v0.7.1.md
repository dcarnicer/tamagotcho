## What's new

- **A card-full notice.** When the move of the device's decks to the microSD
  card stops because the card is full, the boot now ends with a **"Card full"**
  notice: "1 deck stays on the device" / "N decks stay on the device". The
  Decks list keeps a top line too ("Card full: 1 deck on the device"), and
  opening Decks retries the move: free space on the card and the decks move,
  with no reboot and no card reinsert. The strings are singular/plural in
  English and Spanish. The move still keeps every deck on LittleFS when it
  does not fit; nothing is lost.
- **v0.7.1 is the first over-the-air release.** Devices running v0.7.0 can
  install it from **Settings › Device › Firmware**: the image downloads into
  the second app slot, is checked against the release's
  `tamagotcho-firmware.txt`, and settings, decks and progress stay. There is
  no flash-layout change in this release.

**Checked on hardware** (Cardputer ADV, 2026-10-27): a 64 MB FAT32 card with
471 KB free kept two decks and then one, with the boot notice and the deck
list's line; after deleting the fillers, opening Decks moved the deck and
cleared the line. An exFAT partition showed "Card not readable: format it as
FAT32". The release environments build and the native tests pass (302 passed,
1 skipped).
