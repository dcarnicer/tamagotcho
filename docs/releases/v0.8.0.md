## What's new

- **French and Portuguese join the app.** The language picker now offers
  **Auto / English / Español / Français / Português**; Auto follows the loaded
  deck (`en`, `es`, `fr`, `pt`). The quiz, settings, intro card, errors and
  hints are translated, and a new device asks for its language among the four
  on first boot. The portal page remains English/Spanish for now.
- **24 prebuilt decks.** The decks release now carries JLPT N5-N1 and the
  2,136 Jōyō kanji in **English, Spanish, French and Portuguese**. French and
  Portuguese meanings are ordered with WikDict's en-fr/en-pt dictionaries
  (CC BY-SA 4.0), with the same clear-error overrides as Spanish (安, 計, 帰,
  私, 失).
- **Example glosses in French and Portuguese.** The learning mode's intro card
  shows reviewed French/Portuguese glosses for the **N5 and N4** example words
  (146 and 297 words per deck, all glossed), alongside the English and Spanish
  ones. N3-N1 and Jōyō still show the word and reading alone until the next
  review.
- **More useful first meanings.** The N2 and N1 meaning reviews added 24
  hand-checked overrides (N2 9, N1 15) on top of the N5-N4 ones. The N4 Spanish
  example glosses were reviewed too (267 `ok`, 7 `skip`), so the Spanish N4
  intro card now glosses all 297 words.
- **A softer quiz prompt.** A new question's kanji fades in over three 50 ms
  steps instead of appearing at full ink; any press finishes it at once. The
  intro and the detail card keep their stroke-order animation.
- **Two images per platform.** The StickS3/Cardputer ADV build ships
  `tamagotcho-latest.bin` (the whole flash at `0x0`, also for M5Burner and
  M5Launcher) and `tamagotcho-0.8.0-ota.bin` (the app-only image the update
  manifest names). The T-Display-S3 keeps
  `tamagotcho-tdisplay-s3-merged.bin` and `tamagotcho-tdisplay-s3.bin`; the
  old `sticks3` and `cardputer-adv` builds update to this combined image
  through manifest aliases.

**Checked on hardware** (StickS3, 2026-11-18): French and Portuguese as the
device language, the settings and deck list in both, the N4 French/Portuguese
quizzes, and the N5/N4 intro cards with their reviewed glosses. The release
environments build and the firmware's native tests pass (304 cases, 1 skipped);
the pack builder passes 259 tests.

**Decks**: [`decks-v3`](https://github.com/dcarnicer/tamagotcho/releases/tag/decks-v3)
holds the 24 packs and the catalog the device reads. Decks, settings and
progress stay when updating from v0.7.x; the flash layout does not change.
